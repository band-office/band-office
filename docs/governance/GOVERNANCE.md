# Band Office governance

Band Office is an open-source project maintained for school music programs.

## Principles

- School-program usefulness comes before feature count.
- Data ownership, exportability, and operational clarity are product requirements.
- Privacy and permissions are explicit boundaries, not marketing claims.
- A smaller, verified release is better than a broad, implied roadmap.
- Contributors should be able to understand why a decision was made.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers set release gates, merge contributions, steward security and privacy boundaries, and decide when a proposal is ready to enter the roadmap.

### Contributors

Contributors propose, implement, document, test, review, and help other participants. Sustained contributors may be invited into broader triage or maintenance responsibilities as the project grows.

### Users and domain reviewers

Band directors, educators, school staff, self-hosters, and district technology teams provide workflow evidence and acceptance feedback. Domain evidence is especially important when a change affects school-year rollover, family access, inventory custody, fees, forms, communications, or events.

## Decisions

Small, reversible changes can be decided in an issue or pull request. Changes to the data model, privacy boundary, external services, deployment model, release policy, or product scope require an issue-first proposal with:

- the school-program problem;
- affected roles and records;
- the smallest useful change;
- risks and non-goals;
- migration, backup, permission, and testing effects.

Durable product boundaries are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](../product/DECISIONS.md). Final merge and release decisions remain with the maintainers.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes should be proposed publicly and allowed a reasonable review period. Security, legal, or conduct matters may require private handling.
